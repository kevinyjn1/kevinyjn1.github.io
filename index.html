01Research

Two labs, one question: can a model act on what it sees?

May 2026 – now

Surgical video understanding

COG Lab (Corso Group), with Prof. Jason Corso

  • Post-training a Qwen2.5-VL model with DPO, Step-DPO and GRPO, with step-level rewards for step correctness, order and grounding.
  • Benchmarking GPT-5.1, Gemini, Qwen3-VL and InternVL on surgical skill classification against the lab's 70.7% baseline, which I reproduced on a Slurm GPU cluster and fixed a frame-rate bug in.
  • De-identifying surgical video with a SAM 3 segmentation pipeline for a dataset release.

Jan 2025 – now

Robot learning

Extreme Robotics (Atombots) Lab

  • Curated a 90-episode SO-101 manipulation dataset, 40 with mid-grasp disturbances, to study how ACT and SmolVLA policies recover from failure.
  • Co-lead a 5-person team building a multi-camera AprilTag motion-capture system, validated against a ground-truth grid.
  • Building a wheel-legged quadruped with ROS 2 on NVIDIA Jetson.

Liminal Training: Characterizing and Mitigating Subliminal Learning in Large Language Models

Fine-tuning on unrelated data can quietly pass on a trait or a misalignment. We find that this spikes in the first 10–20 training steps, and propose liminal training, an annealed KL regularizer that prevents it. ResponsibleFM workshop, as an Algoverse Fellow.
