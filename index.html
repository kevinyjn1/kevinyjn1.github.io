01 — About

Perception
you can trust.

I research surgical video understanding in the VIGIL Lab under Prof. Jason Corso — building models that perceive the operating room and, just as importantly, know when they might be wrong.

My focus is uncertainty-aware perception: extending surgical hand-pose tracking with conformal prediction and temporal conditioning, and studying how procedural priors from surgical VLMs can make these systems safer. Beyond the OR, I care about multimodal perception, robot learning, and embodied AI.
