About

Seoul → Ann Arbor

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Michigan, graduating in May 2027. I grew up in Seoul, and I do research in two labs.

In Prof. Jason Corso's COG Lab, I work on surgical video understanding: post-training vision-language models with DPO, Step-DPO and GRPO so they follow the steps of an operation, and benchmarking frontier models on surgical skill assessment.

At the Extreme Robotics (Atombots) Lab, I work on robot learning: imitation-learning datasets and policies, robot arms, and a multi-camera motion-capture system.

Liminal Training: Characterizing and Mitigating Subliminal Learning in Large Language Models

Workshop on Socially Responsible and Trustworthy Foundation Models (ResponsibleFM) · Algoverse Fellow

Studies subliminal learning, where fine-tuning on unrelated data quietly transmits a trait or misalignment, finds that trait acquisition spikes in the first 10–20 training steps, and proposes liminal training, an annealed KL regularizer that prevents it.
