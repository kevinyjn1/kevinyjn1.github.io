01What I work on

  1. I

    Robot learning · Atombots Lab

    Robots that learn from people.

    Imitation learning on real arms and a wheel-legged quadruped: a dataset with deliberate mid-grasp failures to test how ACT and SmolVLA recover, a diffusion policy trained on human demonstrations, and the motion capture to measure it all.

    YOLHInside SmolVLAGo2W mocapRectified flowMBot
  2. II

    Surgical video AI · COG Lab

    Models that follow a surgery.

    Post-training Qwen2.5-VL with DPO, Step-DPO and GRPO so it judges surgical steps, benchmarking frontier VLMs on skill classification, and de-identifying video with SAM 3.

    The research →
  3. III

    Embedded ML

    ML small enough for a microcontroller.

    Classifiers that run on the board itself: a bottle that listens to its own water level, and MNIST on a microcontroller fed 5-bit pixels over UART.

NeurIPS 2025 WorkshopPublished

Liminal Training: Characterizing and Mitigating Subliminal Learning in Large Language Models

Fine-tuning on unrelated data can quietly pass on a trait or a misalignment. We find that this spikes in the first 10–20 training steps, and propose liminal training, an annealed KL regularizer that prevents it. ResponsibleFM workshop, as an Algoverse Fellow.
