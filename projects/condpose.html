Uncertainty-Gated CondPose

Most surgical AI models give you a best-guess answer and move on. For hand-pose estimation in the operating room that's not good enough — a wrong, confident prediction downstream of an occluded hand can corrupt a whole tracking sequence. This project extends CondPose with split conformal prediction to give every joint a statistically-guaranteed uncertainty interval, then uses those intervals to gate temporal conditioning.

The core idea

Split conformal prediction works by holding out a calibration set, measuring the model's residual errors on it, and using that empirical distribution to size a prediction region for new inputs. The key property: the region contains the true value at least 1−α of the time, with no distributional assumptions about the model. Swap in any backbone, the guarantee still holds.

The novelty here is making this per-joint and temporal. Different fingers have very different occlusion patterns. A wide interval on the index fingertip is a signal — the model is saying it isn't sure — and that signal can be used to downweight that joint's contribution to the temporal state update.

Status

Active research at the Corso Group (Prof. Jason Corso, University of Michigan). Target venue: SafeSurg or MICCAI workshop. Currently exploring kinematic embedding priors and surgical VLM-derived language priors as additional conditioning signals.

Stack

PyTorch, CondPose backbone, split conformal prediction, LSTM temporal conditioning, CholecT50 surgical video dataset.
