Welcome — and what I'm building this summer

This is the first post on my new site. I wanted a place to write things down that are longer than a tweet but shorter than a paper — half lab notebook, half thinking-out-loud.

This summer I'm in the VIGIL Lab at Michigan working on surgical video understanding. The core question I keep coming back to is deceptively simple: when should a model be allowed to say "I don't know"? In most ML benchmarks a confident wrong answer and an uncertain wrong answer score the same. In an operating room they very much do not.

What's on deck

A few things I expect to write about over the coming weeks:

  • Conformal prediction and why calibrated uncertainty beats raw accuracy in safety-critical settings.
  • What I'm learning about surgical VLMs and procedural priors.
  • The unglamorous reality of getting a MICCAI workshop submission together before July 1.

If any of that sounds interesting, the blog index is the place to follow along. Thanks for reading.
