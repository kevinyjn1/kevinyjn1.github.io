01 — About

Perception you can trust

I research surgical video understanding in the VIGIL Lab under Prof. Jason Corso — building models that perceive the operating room and, just as importantly, know when they might be wrong.

My focus is uncertainty-aware perception: extending surgical hand-pose tracking with conformal prediction and temporal conditioning, and studying how procedural and language priors from surgical VLMs can make these systems safer. More broadly I care about multimodal perception, robot learning, and embodied AI — systems that act reliably under real-world uncertainty.
